# Round 3: decisions

Brief from the client: keep the interactions, but calmer and tidier. Less of everything. No meta commentary in the copy. Brand colours and shapes from cryptoclubvienna.com (pending access, see the end).

## Motion budget: four moments, nothing else moves on its own

| Keep | Change |
|---|---|
| 3D Vienna hero, skyline to plan | fewer packets in flight (1 at a time), slower idle, no beam pulse |
| Ask card typewriter (Xelantum) | slower loop, no chips row, no sheen, no scanning beat |
| Events chain, pending block mines | mining tick 900 ms instead of 260 ms, no travelling packets, no hover scramble |
| Vienna map | draw-in once, U-Bahn off by default, halo only when live |

Cut: loader, side rail (duplicates the nav), magnetic buttons, ScrambleText on headers, width-unfold on headings, proof count-up, lattice sweep on every header, wordmark shimmer, card hover lifts, button arrow nudge.
Kept quiet: manifesto words lighting with scroll (it paces reading), small 16px rise on groups.

## Visual noise

- Section headers: one mono label and a hairline. No "Block 0X", no per-block counts, no full-width lattice strip. The block motif lives in three places only: logo, events chain, map.
- Hero meta: three facts, no coordinates (coordinates stay in the footer only).
- Proof band: three numbers. "0 tips on what to buy" was a joke that reads as copy.
- Mono uppercase only for labels and data; less of it overall.
- Track visuals at lower contrast; only the active one animates.

## Copy: cut the meta commentary

Removed sentences that explain the site to the reader ("Gold pins mark…", "It describes the market. It does not tell you…", "Partner offers on this site are labelled…", "The form goes live at launch…", "about 2 min", "Swipe →", the bot feature table). Kept only what a student needs and what the law needs (Werbung label, risk note, privacy line).

## Brand

Not applied yet: the sandbox cannot open cryptoclubvienna.com. All colours and corner radii are tokens in `site/css/tokens.css`, so the club's palette and shape language replace ours in one file once we can see the site (domain allowed in the environment, or screenshots).
